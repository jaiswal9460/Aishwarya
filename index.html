To Someone Very Special 🌸

I know we first met online in 2023. Back then, I had a small feeling for you, but I wasn't sure what it was.

When you came into my life as a friend, things slowly started changing for me. Your belief in me, your encouragement, and your support gave me confidence during one of the most important phases of my life. I'll always believe you brought good luck into my life because, with your support, I achieved one of my biggest dreams— getting admission to IIT. I'll always be thankful for that.

Over the last two years, I realized that what I felt wasn't just friendship. My feelings for you became stronger with time, and today I can honestly say... I really like you.

I know we come from different states, different cultures, and that relationships like this aren't always easy. But some people are worth taking a chance on, and for me... You're one of them.

Meeting you in Bangalore made me even more certain about my feelings. Those two days were truly special to me. I still remember your beautiful smile, your expressive eyes, and especially your voice. Being with you felt peaceful, and those memories still make me smile.

I'm not asking you to say yes today. I just wanted to tell you how I truly feel. If you're comfortable, I'd love the chance to know you even better and see where life takes us. If you don't feel the same way, I'll respect your feelings completely.

I can't promise a perfect life or that I'll fulfill every dream you have. But I can promise I'll always give my 100% to support your dreams, make your life a little easier whenever I can, and always be someone you can rely on.

I'm just starting my career, and I don't have everything figured out yet. But I'm working hard to build a stable future, a good career, a home, and a meaningful life. I believe that with honesty and hard work, everything else can be built over time.

There's something else I really admire about you. The love and care you have for your mom says a lot about the kind of person you are. If life ever brings us together, I'll always respect and care for your family as my own, and I'll do my best to support them whenever I can.

I'm also trying to build my career in Bangalore, because I genuinely like the city and can imagine building my future there. Maybe one day our paths will meet there again.

Whatever your answer is, thank you for being such an important part of my life. You've inspired me more than you probably realize, and I'll always be grateful for that.

And just one last thing...

"If you ever decide to hold my hand, I'll do my best every single day to make sure you never regret that decision."

Take your time. There is no pressure at all. I just wanted you to know what's in my heart. ❤️
